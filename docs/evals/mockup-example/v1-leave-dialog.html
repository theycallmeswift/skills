Challenge · leave confirmation

v1 · booth-portal

An attendee presses Esc during a challenge. Leaving deletes their progress and sends the station back to Attract. Shown: the top 11 of 24 rows and the status bar, at 80 columns.

A

Pop-up, timer paused, Enter keeps playing

Enter or Esc goes back to the challenge. Only L leaves, so mashing keys can't wipe a run. Downside: if someone walks away with it open, the station waits here until the idle reset.
